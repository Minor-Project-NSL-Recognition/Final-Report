\chapter{CONCLUSION}
\section{Conclusion}
This project successfully developed a Transformer-based OCR system for recognizing handwritten Nepali words in the Devanagari script. The proposed architecture pairs a pre-trained Vision Transformer (ViT-Base) encoder with 
a lightweight custom BERT-based decoder of approximately 4 million parameters, integrated using HuggingFace's VisionEncoderDecoderModel framework.The model was trained on the IIIT-HW-Dev dataset comprising 69,802 word images using a two-phase training strategy. Phase 1 trained only the decoder while keeping the encoder frozen, and Phase 2 performed full end-to-end 
fine-tuning with differential learning rates. The best validation CER of \textbf{6.68\%} was achieved at epoch 52, and final evaluation on the held-out test set yielded a CER of 5.43\%, WER of 16.58\%, and Exact Match Accuracy of 83.42\%. These results demonstrate that the proposed model effectively handles the structural complexity of Devanagari handwriting, including conjunct consonants, vowel diacritics, and inter-writer variability.

\section{Limitations}
Despite the promising results, the system has the following limitations:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The model is trained and evaluated on a single dataset (IIIT-HW-Dev), which may limit its generalization to unseen handwriting styles and real-world scanned documents.
    \item Recognition accuracy decreases for longer words ($\geq$13 characters) due to the increased complexity of conjunct 
    consonant sequences in Devanagari.
    \item The model was trained on Kaggle's NVIDIA Tesla T4 GPU, which limited exploration of larger architectures such as TrOCR-Large.
    \item Confidence scores derived from beam search log-probabilities are approximate and may not reliably reflect true prediction certainty for all word forms.
\end{itemize}

\section{Future works}
Based on the findings of this project, the following works can be achieved in future:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Dataset expansion:} Collect and annotate a larger, more diverse Nepali handwriting dataset covering varied writing styles, age groups, and document types to improve generalization.
    \item \textbf{Line and paragraph recognition:} Extend the system  by integrating a word segmentation module to enable recognition of full lines and paragraphs rather than isolated words.
    \item \textbf{Data augmentation:} Explore more aggressive augmentation strategies such as elastic distortion and synthetic handwriting generation to further improve robustness.
    \item \textbf{Larger decoder:} Investigate a medium-sized BERT-based decoder (4--6 layers) with sufficient GPU resources  to determine whether additional decoder capacity improves accuracy on complex conjunct words.
    \item \textbf{Language model post-processing:} Incorporate a Nepali language model for post-correction of beam search outputs to reduce character-level errors caused by visually similar characters.

\end{itemize}
